Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I get started?

Simply contact us on Telegram or fill out the form above. We'll schedule a free consultation to assess your level and discuss your goals.

How long are the programs?

Programs range from 4 to 12 weeks depending on the course. We also offer flexible scheduling options for working professionals.

What's included in the free consultation?

We'll assess your current English level, discuss your goals, recommend the right program, and answer any questions you have.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, we offer flexible payment options. Details are discussed during your free consultation based on your chosen program.

Can I join if I'm a beginner?

Absolutely! We have programs for all levels, from complete beginners to advanced learners looking to refine their skills.

What if I miss a class?

We offer makeup sessions and provide materials for missed classes. Our flexible scheduling helps accommodate busy professionals.
