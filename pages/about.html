About SewaSew Academy

Building confident English communicators since 2019

Our Mission

To empower professionals, migrants, and business owners in Ethiopia with practical English communication skills that open doors to international opportunities.

Our Vision

To be Ethiopia's most trusted English communication training academy, known for transforming students' confidence and career prospects through practical, results-oriented education.

Our Story

SewaSew Academy was founded in 2019 with a simple observation: many talented professionals in Ethiopia were being held back by their English communication skills, not their lack of ability or expertise.

We saw doctors, engineers, business owners, and ambitious workers who had tremendous potential but couldn't access international opportunities because of language barriers. Traditional English courses weren't helping — they focused on grammar rules and textbook exercises that didn't translate to real-world confidence.

So we created something different. A training academy focused entirely on practical communication — the kind of English you actually use in business meetings, interviews, conferences, and daily professional life.

Today, we've helped over 500 professionals transform their English skills and their careers. Our students work at multinational companies, pursue international education, and run businesses that compete globally.
